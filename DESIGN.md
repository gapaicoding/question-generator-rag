# Design direction — PDF Question Generator

**Product:** a personal/internal utility that turns an uploaded PDF into grounded multiple-choice questions. Not a marketing surface, not a product with brand equity to project.

**Audience:** the owner and anyone they hand this tool to directly. No external visitors, no sales funnel.

**Visual language:** calm, utility-first, closer to GOV.UK/Linear than to a landing page. Function and legibility over decoration. Neutral palette (black/white/zinc), inverted per theme, no accent color beyond the primary action (upload / generate / search buttons).

**Typography:** Geist via `next/font`, system fallback. No special typographic treatment; this is a form-and-list app, not an editorial one.

**Dial:** ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 — a uniform, low-energy, mostly-static interface is the correct choice here, not a fallback.
